I am a comparative literature scholar working on Late Enlightenment German culture.

My first monograph Genre, Race, and the Production of Subjectivity in German Romanticism (2023) examined the influence of racism on aesthetic philosophies of German Romanticism. I finished my PhD at Brown University and have held positions at Brown (Assistant Professor, 2018-19), University of Cambridge (Schroeder Research Fellow, 2019-23), and Rutgers (Visiting Assistant Professor, 2023-24). I live in New Jersey.
